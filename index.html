DEMO / SAMPLE PROJECT

Google Ads Lead Generation Campaign

Comprehensive Search Campaign Planning for Local Home Services

Important Notice: Demo project — campaign structure and data are for portfolio demonstration and are not represented as actual client results.

Project Overview & Objective

This sample project illustrates an end-to-end Google Search Ads strategy built to generate high-intent, qualified inbound leads for a local home-services business (e.g., plumbing, heating, and installations). The objective was to capture users at the exact moment of commercial search intent while minimizing wasted budget.

Challenge

Local home-service queries have high Cost-Per-Click (CPC) and aggressive competition. Broad keyword matching often wastes spend on low-intent DIY queries (e.g., "how to fix a pipe yourself") instead of commercial emergency calls.

Approach & Campaign Structure

Structured tightly into themed ad groups with exact and phrase match types, negative keyword scrub lists, and responsive search ads:

Campaign: Home Services Search (Geo-Targeted) ├── Ad Group 1: Plumbing Services │ ├── [plumber near me] │ ├── "emergency plumber" │ └── "licensed plumbing repair" ├── Ad Group 2: Heating & Boiler │ ├── "heating repair service" │ ├── "boiler installation contractor" │ └── [emergency heating service] └── Ad Group 3: Installation & Replacements ├── "home fixture installation" └── "water heater replacement near me" Negative Keywords: free, diy, jobs, salary, training, courses, pictures, wiki

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs to Monitor)

  • Impressions & Impression Share: Monitoring visibility across target geographic radius.
  • Clicks & Click-Through Rate (CTR): Benchmark targeting 5%–8% for high-intent search ads.
  • Average CPC: Managed via smart bidding target CPA thresholds.
  • Conversions & Conversion Rate: Tracking form submissions and phone call clicks.
  • Cost per Conversion: Ensuring customer acquisition cost remains within client margin.

Tools & Deliverables

Tools: Google Ads Keyword Planner, Google Ads Editor, Google Tag Manager (conversion triggers).

Deliverables: Campaign hierarchy sheet, copy variations matrix, negative keyword master list, bid strategy roadmap.

SAMPLE SQL PROJECT

Healthcare Schema & SQL Query Suite

Relational Database Design, Complex Aggregations & Query Optimization

Relational Database Structure

Designed a Third Normal Form (3NF) relational schema for a multi-tiered district healthcare system tracking facilities, logistics inventories, vaccination batches, and patient records.

-- Sample Analytical Query: Monthly District Cold Chain Adherence SELECT d.district_name, COUNT(c.node_id) AS total_monitored_units, SUM(CASE WHEN c.current_temp BETWEEN 2.0 AND 8.0 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS compliant_units, ROUND( (SUM(CASE WHEN c.current_temp BETWEEN 2.0 AND 8.0 THEN 1.0 ELSE 0 END) / COUNT(c.node_id)) * 100, 2 ) AS adherence_percentage FROM districts d INNER JOIN cold_chain_nodes c ON d.district_id = c.district_id GROUP BY d.district_name HAVING COUNT(c.node_id) > 0 ORDER BY adherence_percentage DESC;

Capabilities Demonstrated

  • Primary & Foreign Key integrity with cascading constraint safety.
  • Multi-table INNER and LEFT JOINs across facilities and inventory logs.
  • Grouping, aggregation, conditional SUM, and subqueries.
  • Window functions (RANK, ROW_NUMBER) for chronological cohort tracking.
